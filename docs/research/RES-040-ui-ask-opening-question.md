# RES-040: The opening question through ui.ask

## Question
Does the opening question use a mod's `$.ui.ask`, with no model turn, in place of a started turn? Test it on every source, with a draft in the box, and with a mid-turn prompt.

## Findings
- Test method: RES-038's method, with these changes. Every "local test" below ran on Claude Code 2.1.318 in a real interactive session, in a throwaway folder that inherited the trust of its parent, with a throwaway mod from `--plugin-dir`, `--setting-sources project` and the haiku model. A Bun PTY driver typed the keys and `@xterm/headless` read the screen, so RES-038's mis-drawn rows did not occur. The oracle was the screen, a hook log of `SessionStart`, `PreToolUse`, `PostToolUse`, `UserPromptSubmit`, `Stop`, `Notification` and the compact hooks, and a log the mod wrote. The mod called `$.ui.ask` on `$.clock.after(0)` and then `$.prompt.submit` with `asUser: true`, and sessions ran in auto mode, unless a line says otherwise. The record rests on 267 runs. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- `$.ui.ask(question, options)` opens "the engine's own AskUserQuestion dialog" and resolves to the chosen label, the labels comma-joined for a multi-select, or the text typed under "Other". It is "a `tool.call` of `AskUserQuestion` through every hook but the calling one", and it "rejects when dismissed, and in a `-p` run". Source: the `ui.ask` doc in the `claude-code.d.ts` that Claude Code 2.1.318 writes, checked 2026-11-05.
- `$.ui.ask` takes 2 to 4 labels, a `header` of at most 12 characters and `multiSelect`. It has no per-option description or preview field, which the `AskUserQuestion` tool has. Source: the `AskOptions` type and the `AskUserQuestion` tool input in the `claude-code.d.ts` that Claude Code 2.1.318 writes, checked 2026-11-05.
- Where the guard loads, a user's mod's `classic.` hooks never run, so `classic.SessionStart` cannot start the question there. The guard's own rule for `classic.*` is `next.to(e, 'append')`. Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/admin#know-which-hooks-of-a-users-mod-dont-run, https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/blob/main/mods/sec-default/hooks/register.ts, checked 2026-11-05.
- The guard's table passes everything else, and it names `prompt.submit`, `tool.call`, `command.run`, `session.*` but `session.append` and `session.compact`, `ui.*` but `ui.log`, and `clock.*`. So by the source `session.start`, `$.ui.ask`, `$.clock.after`, `$.prompt.submit` and `$.command.run` all pass the guard. This answers RES-038's guard line for `$.ui.ask` and `$.command.run` by source, not by a run on a real managed machine. Source: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/tree/main/mods/sec-default, checked 2026-11-05.
- The guard's option `allowManagedModsOnly` refuses every mod a user installed or named with `--plugin-dir`, and a mod from a GitHub, git, URL or npm source always counts as a user's. The docs give no exemption for `$.ui.ask`. Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/admin#stop-user-installed-mods-from-loading, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/admin#install-your-organizations-mods-and-set-the-order, checked 2026-11-05.
- After the user stops a turn, `$.prompt.submit` resolves with a `drop` and `$.command.run` rejects, until the user's next input. A mod's `session.start` that runs again in a fresh copy does not repeat a `$.prompt.submit`, `$.command.run` or `$.agent.spawn` call; the docs do not name `$.ui.ask` in that rule. Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/api#calls-and-background-work-after-the-user-interrupts-a-turn, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/troubleshoot#its-sessionstart-ran-again-in-a-fresh-copy, checked 2026-11-05.
- From `classic.SessionStart`, the dialog opened and the answer reached the agent on every source: `startup`, `clear`, `compact`, `resume`, `fork`, `/branch`, `--continue` and in-session `/resume`, 3 of 3 runs each, 24 of 24. No model choice is involved. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- From `session.start`, the dialog opened and the answer reached the agent on `startup`, `--resume`, `--continue` and `--resume --fork-session`, 3 of 3 runs each, 12 of 12. On `/clear`, `/compact`, `/branch` and in-session `/resume` no dialog opened, 0 of 12, while `classic.SessionStart` fired in all 12. `session.start` fired once per process in all 24 runs. Source: local test, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/reference#session, checked 2026-11-05.
- The dialog stood on screen 0.64 to 1.1 seconds after launch on the process sources, with four sessions running at once. On in-session sources it stood 0.1 to 0.5 seconds after `classic.SessionStart`. Measured from Enter, that is 0.61 to 0.65 seconds on `/clear` and `/branch`, and 6.3 to 10.2 seconds on `/compact`, most of which is the compaction itself, 3 runs each. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- A conversation that holds earlier answers changes nothing. With no source filter, 3 of 3 runs opened the dialog in turn on `startup`, `/branch`, `/compact` and `/clear` in one process, then on `--continue` and in-session `/resume` in a new process on that conversation, and the agent replied to each answer. This is the case where RES-038's `asyncRewake` agent skipped the question in 9 of 9 runs. Source: local test, docs/research/RES-038-opening-question.md:28, checked 2026-11-05.
- The hold-back that RES-038 saw still applies with no model turn. When keys run on through the moment `$.ui.ask` is called, the screen shows "Waiting for permission…", the keys stay in the prompt box, and the dialog opens 1.38 to 1.5 seconds after the last key, 29 of 29 runs, however long after the call that is. `PreToolUse` for `AskUserQuestion` fires at the call, before the dialog shows. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- A draft typed and left unsent before the dialog survives it, 3 of 3 runs. The dialog opened over the box, the answer reached the agent as its own turn, and "fix bug" was still in the prompt box afterwards. `$.prompt.read()` gave the mod the draft at the call: `{ text: "fix bug", cursor: 7 }`. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- A stray Enter answers the dialog, 3 of 3 runs each way. Enter 0.4 seconds after the dialog opened picked "Active item". A digit picked its row at once. Letters did nothing and showed nowhere, and Enter after them picked "Active item". Space did nothing. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- A draft, a pause and more keys answer by accident, as in RES-038. "fix", then a pause until the dialog opened, then " the login bug" and Enter answered "Active item", 3 of 3 runs. The later letters were lost, and "fix" stayed in the prompt box. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- The time a user starts to type decides the outcome, with the mod's timer at 0. "fix the bug" and Enter, started 0.1, 0.3 or 0.5 seconds after launch, went to the prompt box and ran as the user's prompt in 9 of 9 runs, and the dialog opened after it. The same keys started 0.7, 1.0 or 1.5 seconds after launch met the open dialog: the text was lost, no prompt ran, and Enter answered "Active item" in 9 of 9 runs. In 1 of the 9 early runs the prompt lost a key, as "fixthe bug". Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- The dialog drops keys for a short time after it opens. On a plain open, a digit or Enter up to 0.1 seconds after the dialog showed on screen did nothing, 0 of 11 runs, and from 0.15 seconds on it was taken, 21 of 21 runs. After a hold-back the window is longer: a key up to 0.3 seconds after the dialog showed did nothing, 0 of 10 runs, and from 0.6 seconds on it was taken, 16 of 16 runs. A dropped key leaves the dialog open and picks nothing, and a later press works. In all 26 runs of this hold-back test the draft stayed in the prompt box. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- A first prompt sent before the dialog, with the mod's timer at 6 seconds, ran as its own turn and got its reply. The dialog then opened on its timer, the answer reached the agent as a second turn, and nothing was lost, 3 of 3 runs. As in RES-038, the user is asked what to work on just after asking for something. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- `$.ui.ask` does not wait for a running turn: a call during a turn opened the dialog at once over it, with `Bash(sleep 9)` still running on the row above, 6 of 6 runs. The turn went on behind the dialog. In 3 of those runs the tool finished, the agent replied "PROMPT-SEEN" and the turn ended while the dialog stayed open. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
- An answer given during a running turn waits: `$.prompt.submit` ran it as a turn of its own once the session was idle, and both replies came, in the order "PROMPT-SEEN", then "ANSWER-SEEN: A bug", 6 of 6 runs. Nothing was lost. This is the documented rule: the call "waits until the session is idle and then starts a new turn". Source: local test, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/api#start-a-turn-from-a-background-job, checked 2026-11-05.

The answer text reached the agent in all 12, and no text was lost; in the 6 joined runs the agent's reply followed the later prompt. Source: local test, checked 2026-11-05.
