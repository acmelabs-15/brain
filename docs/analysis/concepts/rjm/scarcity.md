## Consumes
Authentic capacity constraints, fixed inventory, or bounded customer onboarding slots.

## Produces
Elevated perceived value, heightened desire, and immediate purchasing commitment.

## When applied
Applied when supply or delivery bandwidth is legitimately restricted and buyers must act promptly to secure participation.

## Design notes
A psychological and offer amplifier technique in rjm's business strategy tools derived from both Hormozi's offer design and Cialdini's influence taxonomy. It leverages natural supply limitations to spur action, while emphasizing that fabricated scarcity destroys brand trust and violates consumer protections.
