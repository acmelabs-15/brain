## Consumes
Pre-existing audience such as contact lists, social followers, past clients, or personal network contacts.

## Produces
Immediate qualified conversations, fast customer feedback, and low-cost closed sales.

## When applied
Applied when an audience already exists and the business requires rapid pipeline expansion without upfront advertising capital.

## Design notes
One of the Core Four lead acquisition channels in rjm's business strategy toolkit. Warm outreach conducts direct 1-to-1 conversations with individuals who already know the seller, providing the fastest and cheapest empirical validation of an offer before deploying cold or paid channels.
