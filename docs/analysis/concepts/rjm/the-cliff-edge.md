## Design notes
`The Cliff Edge` represents the perilous asymmetrical boundary between Clear and Chaotic. When teams treat Clear solutions as permanent and cease questioning assumptions, inevitable environmental shifts bypass gradual degradation and pitch the system directly into catastrophic chaos.
