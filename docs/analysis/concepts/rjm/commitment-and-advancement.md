## Design notes
Commitment and advancement serves as the reality filter in rjm's customer discovery. Because praise is cost-free and misleading, this rule forces founders to demand skin in the game (time, reputation, or money) at the conclusion of every interview, separating genuine commercial traction from polite conversation.
