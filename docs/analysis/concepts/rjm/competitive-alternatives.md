## Design notes
Competitive alternatives form the baseline for positioning in Obviously Awesome. Rather than comparing against direct software rivals alone, this concept forces teams to identify what the customer would realistically do if the product vanished (often manual effort or spreadsheets), establishing the true baseline against which unique value must be proven.
