## Consumes
Advertising budget, tested creative and copy, target audience parameters, and complete funnel analytics instrumentation.

## Produces
Immediate stranger traffic at volume, measurable customer acquisition cost (CAC), and scalable lead flow.

## When applied
Applied when capital is available but organic reach is lacking, and the underlying offer has demonstrated baseline conversion.

## Design notes
The 1-to-many outbound paid channel in rjm's Core Four acquisition framework. Paid advertising provides the fastest mechanism to buy stranger attention and scale customer volume, but demands rigorous conversion tracking and proven unit economics to avoid exhausting capital.
