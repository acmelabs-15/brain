## Design notes
Concrete prevents communication breakdown caused by abstract jargon in rjm's strategy references. By enforcing that concepts be expressed in terms of human actions and sensory perceptions rather than abstract corporate metrics, it enables diverse team members and customers to construct identical mental models.
