## Design notes
The centerpiece offer-design technique in rjm's business strategy library. A Grand Slam Offer constructs an asymmetric commercial proposition that solves every obstacle between the prospect and their dream outcome, stacks named deliverables, and reverses buyer risk to render competitor price comparisons irrelevant.
