## Design notes
The fundamental acquisition taxonomy in rjm's business strategy framework. It simplifies market engagement into four archetypal channels spanning warm versus cold relationships and 1-to-1 versus 1-to-many communication, forcing founders to focus on channel mastery and execution quotas rather than chasing novelty.
