## Design notes
A standardized vendor evaluation instrument in rjm designed to expose third-party vulnerabilities prior to contractual commitment. By quantifying financial stability, maturity, and pricing predictability, it prevents engineering teams from adopting precarious commercial dependencies that pose sudden end-of-life or lock-in risks.
