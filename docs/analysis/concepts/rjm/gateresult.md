## Design notes
`GateResult` is the verdict artifact returned by rjm's optimization gate. It records whether an edit was accepted or rejected, details the exact score delta and statistical significance, and tracks whether the comparison was executed or short-circuited by a refusal guard, preventing unmetered consumption of held-out evaluations.
