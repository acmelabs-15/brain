## Design notes
Restraining Forces captures the systemic obstacles, frictions, and organizational inertia that resist process improvements. In Kurt Lewin's force field model as adapted for agents, reducing restraining forces is often more effective than simply increasing driving pressure when overcoming recurring anti-patterns.
