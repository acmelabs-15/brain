## Design notes
Architect synthesis panels are comprehensive evaluation artifacts generated by the architect agent to consolidate multi-perspective technical debates into actionable design verdicts. In rjm, these panels ensure that complex trade-offs across performance, security, and governance are weighed systematically, providing a transparent and binding record that governs whether proposed changes may proceed to implementation.
