## Consumes
A stated implied need or operational issue admitted by the prospect.

## Produces
Expanded perception of risk, cost, and missed revenue that justifies commercial action.

## When applied
Applied when a buyer admits a difficulty but minimizes its importance or shrugs off its priority.

## Design notes
Implication questions explore the second- and third-order systemic costs of an unresolved problem. In rjm's sales reference, this stage is identified as the single most critical differentiator in high-value B2B deals, transforming a low-priority grievance into an executive-level mandate.
