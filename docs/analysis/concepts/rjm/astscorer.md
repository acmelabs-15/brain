## Design notes
`AstScorer` is an anticipated concrete scorer in rjm's evaluation harness. Conceived for abstract syntax tree validation of model-generated code, its specification serves as the design rationale for structuring `ScoringEngine` as a pluggable Strategy pattern, ensuring new structural assertion types can be introduced without modifying the scoring coordinator or fixture schemas.
