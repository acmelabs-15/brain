## Design notes
A strategic reference framework in rjm derived from W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne's work. It guides founders away from bloody head-to-head competition in established red oceans toward uncontested blue oceans, achieving value innovation by simultaneously raising buyer value and lowering internal operational costs.
