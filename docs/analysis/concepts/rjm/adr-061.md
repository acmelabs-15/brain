## Design notes
ADR-061 proposed refactoring tool-use hook matcher shims to delegate to a canonical implementation body (_impl/invoke_X.py) to prevent cross-matcher drift. Following a six-agent debate that exposed the proposal as premature abstraction with high opportunity cost and latent drift risks, it was formally withdrawn and preserved as institutional knowledge in favor of deterministic full-tree regeneration (Alternative B).
