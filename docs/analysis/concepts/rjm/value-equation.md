## Consumes
Analysis of the customer's dream outcome, perceived likelihood of success, time to result, and required effort/sacrifice.

## Produces
Targeted offer enhancements that systematically increase the value numerator while shrinking the time and effort denominators.

## When applied
Applied when prospects object to price or when optimizing an offer's perceived value relative to alternatives.

## Design notes
A core value-modeling technique in rjm's offer design toolkit. It models perceived value as the product of Dream Outcome and Perceived Likelihood divided by Time Delay and Effort/Sacrifice, instructing founders to elevate perceived value by minimizing buyer friction and delivery time rather than cutting price.
