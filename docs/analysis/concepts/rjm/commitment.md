## Consumes
A prior public stance, verbal agreement, or micro-commitment made by the prospect.

## Produces
A follow-up request framed as the natural, consistent continuation of the counterparty's prior behavior.

## Design notes
Commitment relies on the human psychological drive to maintain behavioral and cognitive consistency with past choices and public positions. In rjm's strategy toolkit, it allows communicators to guide prospects progressively across conversion milestones by framing each step as coherent with earlier decisions.
