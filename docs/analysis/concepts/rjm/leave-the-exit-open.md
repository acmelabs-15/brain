## Produces
Communication explicitly affirming the counterparty's unconditional autonomy to decline.

## When applied

## Design notes
Leave the exit open utilizes the "but you are free" technique from social psychology to defuse psychological reactance. In rjm's design, explicitly granting the recipient the liberty to say no reduces resistance and builds durable trust.
