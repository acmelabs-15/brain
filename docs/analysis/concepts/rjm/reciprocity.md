## Consumes
Genuine value, favor, or gift provided upfront with no contractual or explicit strings attached.

## Produces
Psychological goodwill and inclination by the recipient to reciprocate upon receiving a subsequent request.

## When applied
"If the other side owes you nothing and there is no relationship yet, then lead with Reciprocity: give first, with no strings named." — .claude/skills/business-strategy/references/influence.md:9

## Design notes
Reciprocity leverages the universal human tendency to return favors by delivering tangible, unasked-for value before initiating a business request. In rjm's influence guidance, it mandates that upfront value must be completely unconditional and authentic, warning that artificial favors read as manipulative transactions.
