## Design notes
In addy, the AI aesthetic is an explicit anti-pattern cataloging the lazy, recognizable design choices made by generative models (purple/indigo palettes, gratuitous gradients, uniform maximum border radii, generic hero blocks, and stock card grids). Frontend UI engineering exists specifically to replace these defaults with intentional, production-grade design decisions.
