## Design notes
An optional section within a learning record that translates newly established learner knowledge into actionable pedagogical constraints and opportunities for future sessions. By documenting what topics are now unlocked or ruled out, it ensures subsequent sessions avoid redundant instruction and navigate directly to appropriately challenging material.
