## Design notes
The explicit design choice of where to draw the boundary between a module's internal implementation and its external callers. In Design It Twice, seam placement is treated as its own first-class evaluation criterion—distinct from module depth and change locality—to ensure interfaces are drawn where boundaries naturally occur.
