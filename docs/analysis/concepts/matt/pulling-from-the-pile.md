## Design notes
Pulling from the pile defines the core extraction technique of treating raw authoring material as a quarry rather than a rigid outline. Material can be freely split, recombined, or paraphrased to serve the article's voice, while missing ideas are explicitly called out as gaps to be filled or cut.
