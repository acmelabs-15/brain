## Design notes
Fluency is the cognitive illusion of mastery experienced while effortlessly reading clear explanations. Matt's teach skill explicitly identifies fluency as an anti-goal: unless lessons demand active retrieval and effortful recall, fluency masks the lack of actual long-term retention.
