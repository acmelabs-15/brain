## Consumes
A fuzzy plan, feature proposal, codebase context, or architectural dilemma.

## Produces
Shared alignment, persistent CONTEXT.md vocabulary entries, and recorded ADRs in docs/adr/.

## When applied
Invoked at the beginning of a feature change or architectural design session in an existing repository.

## Design notes
The stateful engineering entry point in matt's lifecycle. It marries the interrogative grilling interview with active domain modeling, ensuring that as decisions and vocabulary are settled, they are written directly to repository files on disk.
