## Consumes
A proposed plan, design decision, or fuzzy idea.

## Produces
Clarified decisions, resolved assumptions, and populated design frontiers across structured interview rounds.

## When applied
Automatically invoked by model-driven workflows or directly by users to interrogate plans before execution.

round template, horizontal rule

## Design notes
The core interrogative interview loop in matt's package. It models problem spaces as design trees, questioning users along the decision frontier in batched rounds to eliminate unstated assumptions and establish firm consensus before code is written.
