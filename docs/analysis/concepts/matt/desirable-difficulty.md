## Design notes
Desirable difficulty is the pedagogical principle that learning tasks should be intentionally challenging to deepen encoding. The teach skill applies difficulty strategically: keeping initial knowledge acquisition frictionless to preserve working memory, while introducing calibrated difficulty during quizzes and drills to solidify storage strength.
