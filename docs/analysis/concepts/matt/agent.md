## Design notes
The core operational role in matt representing the AI coding assistant (such as Claude Code) executing skills, interrogating requirements, writing code, and conducting reviews. In matt's design, the agent is treated as a process-following collaborator that excels at mechanical execution and verification when provided with explicit constraints and clear ubiquitous language, while the human developer retains ultimate ownership of scope and business decisions.
