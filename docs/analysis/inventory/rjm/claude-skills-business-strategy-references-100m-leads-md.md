## Purpose — required, verbatim
> "Use this when you have something to sell but not enough people seeing or wanting it. The decision cue: you can name a product and a price, but your weekly count of qualified conversations is low, erratic, or unknown." — .claude/skills/business-strategy/references/100m-leads.md:7

## Design intent — required
Provides an actionable lead-generation reference distilled from Alex Hormozi's "$100M Leads" for founders who have a defined product and price but lack qualified conversation volume. It introduces the "Core Four" acquisition channels (warm outreach, free content, cold outreach, paid ads), a step-by-step framework to build a Grand Slam Offer lead magnet, quotas for high-volume execution before optimization, "Lead Getters" recruitment, and an 11-point scored checklist. Without this reference, early-stage founders faced with stalled lead flow often jump aimlessly between unmeasured marketing tactics, prematurely tweak already functional offers, or fail due to insufficient outreach activity rather than wrong channel selection.

## Inputs — required
- Founder symptom: product and price exist, but weekly count of qualified conversations is low, erratic, or unknown.
- Funnel metrics: leads per week, cost per lead, lead-to-sale rate.
- Available founder resources: audience size, cash budget, available time.
- Offer status: functioning Grand Slam Offer / lead magnet.
