## Purpose — required, verbatim
> "Systematic identification, documentation, and mitigation of security threats." — .claude/skills/threat-modeling/SKILL.md:10

## Design intent — required
Provides a structured methodology for engineering teams and AI agents to systematically identify, document, and mitigate security threats across four iterative phases aligned with the OWASP Four-Question Framework: Phase 1 (Scope and Decompose: defining boundaries, data flow diagrams, assets, and trust boundaries), Phase 2 (Threat Identification: applying STRIDE per architecture element and building threat matrices), Phase 3 (Mitigation Strategy: calculating Likelihood × Impact risk ratings, selecting mitigation strategies, and generating mitigation roadmaps), and Phase 4 (Validation: running automated verification scripts and conducting peer reviews). Without this skill, security reviews would be ad-hoc, prone to omitting threat categories (such as Repudiation or Elevation of Privilege), lacking quantifiable risk prioritization, or failing to track high-severity mitigations to completion.
