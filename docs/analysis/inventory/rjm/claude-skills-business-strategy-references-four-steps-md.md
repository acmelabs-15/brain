# .claude/skills/business-strategy/references/four-steps.md

## Purpose — required, verbatim
> "A startup is not a small version of a big company. It is a search for a business model that works. This skill applies Steve Blank's Customer Development method to that search. The goal of the early steps is learning, not revenue. You leave the building, test your guesses against real buyers, and refuse to scale until the evidence says the model holds." — .claude/skills/business-strategy/references/four-steps.md:3-7

## Design intent — required
Distills Steve Blank's Customer Development methodology from "The Four Steps to the Epiphany" for founders transitioning from product conception to repeatable sales. It operationalizes the first two steps—Customer Discovery (testing problem hypotheses by "leaving the building" to interview 15–30 prospects without pitching, then testing rough solutions for real buying signals) and Customer Validation (founder-led, manual sales to un-affiliated strangers to prove repeatable commercial viability)—before allowing scaling into Customer Creation and Company Building. Without this reference, early-stage ventures prematurely execute growth playbooks, spend capital on marketing and sales teams, and scale operations before validating that a real market problem and paying demand exist.

## Inputs — required
- Falsifiable hypotheses: target customer persona, problem definition, current expenditure/alternatives, solution value proposition.
- In-person/call interview responses from 15–30 prospective buyers.
- Solution prototypes (mockup, demo, concierge).
