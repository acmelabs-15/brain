> "A reference for choosing how to acquire customers." — .claude/skills/business-strategy/references/traction.md:3

## Design intent — required
Distills Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares's Traction into an objective methodology for selecting customer acquisition channels using the Bullseye method. It counters the tendency of founders to default to familiar marketing channels by requiring a systematic brainstorm across all 19 traction channels, ranking them into Outer, Middle, and Inner rings, running cheap parallel experiments on the top candidates to measure cost-per-customer, volume, and audience fit, and committing resources to a single winning channel until saturation. Without it, early-stage ventures frequently die from lack of distribution or squander capital across fragmented, unvalidated marketing efforts.
