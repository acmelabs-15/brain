## Purpose — required, verbatim
> "Delete the equality raise. Keep `comparison.head`'s existing precedence. Convert the disagreement from a hard failure into a non-blocking diagnostic carried on the returned binding." — .agents/architecture/ADR-102-session-qa-binding-field-precedence.md:132

## Design intent — required
Resolves an architectural conflict between two distinct contracts in session log validation: `endingCommit` (which records a session's own final authored commit, updated during follow-up commits or rebases) and `episodeMetrics.comparison.head` (which is advanced during QA rebinding to bind evidence to a fresh commit after subsequent branch changes). Previously, `session_qa_binding()` enforced strict field equality between these two full 40-character SHAs by raising a `ValueError`, which falsely treated divergence as log corruption and forced operators into manual hand-synchronization churn or workarounds (such as introducing `commitHead` to preserve author provenance). By eliminating the blocking `ValueError` in favor of documented precedence for `comparison.head` and propagating disagreements as non-blocking diagnostics in `QaBinding.inconsistency` (surfaced as `result.warnings` in `validate_qa_report_evidence()`), the architecture allows `endingCommit` and `comparison.head` to independently fulfill their respective lifecycles without false-positive validation failures, preserving audit visibility without blocking downstream QA checks.
