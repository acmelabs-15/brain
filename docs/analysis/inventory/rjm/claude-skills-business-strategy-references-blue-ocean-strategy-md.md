## Purpose — required, verbatim
> "A reference for deciding whether to fight for share in a known market (a red ocean) or create a new market with little competition (a blue ocean), and how to design the move so the new space is hard to copy." — .claude/skills/business-strategy/references/blue-ocean-strategy.md:3

## Design intent — required
Distills W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne's "Blue Ocean Strategy" for founders and strategists evaluating whether to compete in crowded markets ("red oceans") or create uncontested market space ("blue oceans"). It details the strategy canvas, non-customer discovery, the Four Actions framework (Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create), value curves, and the crucial test of value innovation (raising buyer value while cutting cost simultaneously). Without this reference, founders routinely fall into the trap of competing head-on on established industry benchmarks ("like X but better/cheaper"), entering destructive price wars or pursuing differentiation that drives up cost without unlocking new demand.
