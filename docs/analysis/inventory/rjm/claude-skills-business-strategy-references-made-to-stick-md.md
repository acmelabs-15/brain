## Purpose — required, verbatim

## Design intent — required
Provides an operational distillation of Chip and Dan Heath's Made to Stick, framing message memorability as an engineering problem solvable through the six-attribute SUCCESs framework (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories) rather than relying on creative talent. It offers a decision tree to address specific audience gaps, a 10-point scored checklist to test whether an idea can be repeated by a stranger, and guidelines for overcoming the Curse of Knowledge. Without it, messages and technical findings may be factual yet quickly forgotten, failing to persuade or spread.
