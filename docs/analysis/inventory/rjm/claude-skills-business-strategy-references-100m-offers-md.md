## Design intent — required
Synthesizes Alex Hormozi's "$100M Offers" framework into a structured method for designing differentiated, high-ticket offers that escape commodity price wars. It guides founders through targeting starving markets, pricing aggressively based on value rather than cost-plus calculations, maximizing perceived value across the four levers of the Value Equation (dream outcome, perceived likelihood, time delay, effort/sacrifice), turning customer obstacles into deliverables, trimming and stacking named value, and amplifying with real scarcity, urgency, bonuses, and risk-reversal guarantees. Without this reference, founders struggling with price resistance mistakenly discount their products rather than addressing the underlying perceived value deficit.
