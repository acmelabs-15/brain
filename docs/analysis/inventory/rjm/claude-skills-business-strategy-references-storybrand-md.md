## Design intent — required
Distills Donald Miller's Building a StoryBrand into a 7-part narrative messaging framework designed to clarify product communication. It positions the customer as the hero with a specific desire and multi-layered problem (external, internal, philosophical), cast the company as the empathetic and authoritative guide who provides a clear plan, issues direct and transitional calls to action, highlights stakes (failure avoided), and describes the concrete success state. Without it, product marketing copy frequently centers on company awards and technical specifications rather than the customer's emotional drivers, resulting in high bounce rates and flat conversion.
