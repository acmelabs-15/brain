## Purpose — required, verbatim

## Design intent — required
Provides a curated reference catalog of creative problem-solving and lateral thinking frameworks (SCAMPER, How Might We, First Principles, Jobs to Be Done, Constraint-Based Ideation, Pre-mortem, Analogous Inspiration) to assist an ideation agent during Phase 1 (divergent expansion) and Phase 2 (evaluative stress-testing). Without it, an ideation agent risks generating incremental or superficial variations, failing to reframe entrenched assumptions or identify non-obvious solution spaces.
